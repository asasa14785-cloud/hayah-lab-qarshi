## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies **Quiet Medical Authority & Human Reassurance**. Serving diagnostic patients and healthcare professionals in Egypt with an Arabic-first digital touchpoint, the interface rejects cold techno-medical sterility and chaotic commercial banners. Instead, it balances rigorous clinical accuracy with empathetic community warmth.

The emotional arc flows deliberately: **Uncertainty → Clarity → Trust → Action → Reassurance**.

### Design Movement & Visual Language
The aesthetic merges **Contemporary Clinical Minimalism** with **Laboratory Glass Materiality**. UI layers behave like optical instruments: polished silica glass slides, calibrated hairline divisions, high-transparency panels, and balanced breathing room. Every surface, stroke, and spatial offset echoes the disciplined precision of a certified pathology laboratory.

## Colors

### Chromatic Roles
- **Primary (`#0A2540`)**: Deep Medical Blue. Projects uncompromised clinical competence, institutional gravitas, and optical grounding for core calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#0E7490`)**: Controlled Medical Teal. Represents biochemical precision, diagnostics, and sub-category markers without competing against primary blue.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Restrained Health Green. Dedicated to diagnostic normalcy, validated test status badges, and rapid home visit scheduling confirmations.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Blue-Black Obsidian. Delivers crisp legibility against bright optical white surfaces.

### Surface System
The light canvas (`#F8FAFC`) anchors content without harsh glare. Deep inverse sections (`#0B192C`) encapsulate the sticky mobile bottom dock and high-focus footer sections, providing high visual impact for immediate contact pathways.

## Typography

### Typographic Principles (RTL First)
The typography is driven by **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**, selected for its geometric legibility, structural clarity, and high-precision performance across mixed medical reports containing both Latin pharmaceutical terminology and Arabic nomenclature.

### Rules & Formatting
- **Readability Over Decoration**: Never apply full capital styling to Latin chemical formula abbreviations or patient identifiers.
- **RTL Numerical Balancing**: Laboratory numerical parameters and reference ranges (`e.g., 4.5 - 11.0 × 10^3/µL`) preserve directional isolation using standard Unicode directional isolation marks (`dir="ltr"` on numeric ranges inside Arabic cards).
- **Mobile Defensive Scaling**: Headlines contract on mobile viewports to prevent line-wrapping from breaking medical sentence structures.

## Layout & Spacing

### Spatial Philosophy & Grid Architecture
The layout uses an 8px base rhythm with 4px half-steps for compact clinical controls. 

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column asymmetric grid. Accommodates split balances (`7/5` for inquiry input vs live status tracking, `8/4` for test catalogue exploration vs preparation requirements). Maximum container constraint: `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 24px gutters. Secondary sidebars collapse into sticky bottom trays.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single-column flow with 16px lateral safety margins. Crucially incorporates a `5rem` bottom padding buffer (`dock-safe-bottom`) across all pages to prevent sticky quick-booking glass docks from occluding content.

## Elevation & Depth

### Physical Metaphor: Precision Laboratory Glass
Depth is achieved using translucent optical layering, microscopic hairlines, and ambient clinical light dispersion. Heavy black drop shadows are strictly forbidden.

### Depth Tiers
- **Elevation 0 (Flush Canvas)**: Standard content sections, information listings. Border: none. Shadow: none.
- **Elevation 1 (Specimen Base)**: White solid surface panels (`#FFFFFF`) sitting on `#F8FAFC`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.02)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Elevation 2 (Floating Test Card / Glass Panel)**: Translucent surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.07), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.03)`. Top edge highlight: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
- **Elevation 3 (Modal & Diagnostic Query Overlay)**: Surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.96)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.14)`. Outline: 1px solid `rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.18)`.
- **Sticky Glass Dock (Mobile Action Shelf)**: Positioned fixed bottom. Surface: `rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border top: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

### Corner Curvature Logic
Geometric radii follow Level 2 (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline).
- **Forms & Inline Fields (`rounded-md`, 8px)**: Standard inputs, phone inquiry fields, and interactive list elements.
- **Surface Cards (`rounded-lg`, 16px)**: Test package containers, home visit forms, and information banners.
- **Glass Shells & Floating Drawers (`rounded-xl`, 24px)**: Hero callouts and high-elevation modals.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Reserved strictly for micro status indicators (e.g., "متاح سحب منزلي", "جاهز للاستلام") and circular floating communication buttons. Full-pill treatment is never applied to square diagnostic grids.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Medical CTA**: Solid Deep Medical Blue (`#0A2540`) with text in `#FFFFFF`. On hover: `#144D82`. Transitions use `transform: scale(0.99)` on active press.
- **Secondary Glass Action**: Translucent white backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`) with a hairline border (`rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.15)`) and text in `#0A2540`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Access Action**: Pill or rounded-lg element finished in `#25D366` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` icon and Arabic label ("تواصل فوري مع أخصائي المعمل").

### 2. Form Controls & Diagnostic Inputs
- **Search & Input Fields**: White surface background with 1px border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`). On focus: transitions to medical teal border (`#0E7490`) with an outer halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.12)`. Label placed top-right in RTL layout.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom 20px squares/circles with 1.5px border. Checked state fills with `#0A2540` displaying a white checkmark icon.

### 3. Diagnostic Cards & Asymmetric Modules
- Standard test cards feature an asymmetric split: the right side (primary RTL entry) holds the Arabic test title, clinical code, and sample requirements (e.g., "صيام 8 ساعات"). The left side contains price metadata, turnaround duration, and a clean booking trigger.

### 4. Patient Result Tracking Bar
- High-clarity inquiry component: receipt ID field + phone number verification. Employs large tabular typography with clear instructional microcopy and immediate error prevention states.

### 5. Sticky Mobile Action Dock
- Persistent bottom bar pinned at `z-index: 50` on mobile viewports. Hosts quick dual-action triggers: "طلب سحب منزلي" (Home Collection) alongside a direct WhatsApp/Phone consultation shortcut.
